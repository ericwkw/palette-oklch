    Ramps

    Hover a swatch for its step and value, click it to copy, click again to nudge it. Every ramp shares the same lightness steps, so a shade means the same weight whatever the hue, and chroma peaks near the middle and eases off at both ends.

    Dark mode

    Same hues, same step order: 50 stays the lightest. The curve lifts the deep end and eases the light end, so dark surfaces stay separable.

    Roles & tokens

    Roles name what a colour is for; tokens are what a product uses. Ramp steps are the primitives underneath, so a palette swap never changes a token name.

    Where your brand colours land

    Pinning a hex into a ramp is not the same as a token using it. This is the check: does the exact colour you were given appear anywhere a product would actually draw it?

    Which step each token uses

    These decide the primary button, the tinted surface behind secondary and accent, the hairline on every border and input, the focus ring, and the five chart series. Change one and watch the row below, the preview and the export move with it. A step marked your brand colour is the one your pasted hex landed on.

    Brand roles

    Functional colours

    Meaning stays constant across a brand family — these hues do not move when a sister brand changes its main colour. Each one carries a surface, border, text and solid fill.

    States

    Contrast

    WCAG 2 ratios with APCA alongside. WCAG needs 4.5 for body text and 3.0 for large text or UI edges; APCA Lc 60 is roughly body text, Lc 45 large text.

    Token pairs

    Every pair the tokens actually put together, at the level each one needs: 4.5 for text, 3.0 for edges and marks.

    Text on surfaces

    Colour vision

    Status colours that differ only by hue can collapse into one another. These are your own colours, put through the cone response of each kind of colour blindness.

    Solid fills with their own label

    Opacity & overlays

    Drop an image in and the worst case is measured from its own pixels. A colour at an alpha over a known background composites to an exact solid — shown here so a tint can be matched back to a real ramp step; over a photograph there is no single background.

    Alpha over surfaces

    Scrims over an image

    With an image, drag the box to where the caption will really sit. No image loaded uses a light-and-dark test pattern; with one, the pixels inside the box are measured by percentile, so one glint of sunlight does not decide the scrim for the whole picture.

    Gradients

    Interpolated in OKLCH, so a blend between two hues keeps its chroma instead of passing through grey. Copy the CSS under each.

    Preview

    One dense screen — sidebar, table with statuses, a form with an error and a disabled field, a chart on the chart tokens, text at three sizes — drawn only from tokens. Change a mapping and it moves here.

    Brand family

    One parent and its sisters. A sister sets its own main hue and colourfulness — it may travel a long way — and takes everything else from the parent: the same neutrals, the same six functional colours, the same lightness steps, ramp shape and token mapping. That is what makes them read as one family.
